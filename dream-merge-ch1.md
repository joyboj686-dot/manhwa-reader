# Dream Merge — Chapter 1: "The Night The Dream Woke Up"

**Title:** Dream Merge
**Author:** Joy (idea by the user, story by Muse)
**Genre:** Action / System / Drama
**Status:** Original — all characters are adults. Aarav born 2000, Kabir born 2004, both adults in the present-day story.

## Characters
- **Aarav (26)** — MC. Village boy, born 2000. Farmer's son. Topped his board exam, failed higher secondary once, passed with a B after 2 years, barely passed university while gaming, reading manhwa and watching anime.
- **Kabir (22)** — Aarav's younger brother, born on a rainy night in 2004. Fell very ill as a baby, was hospitalised, recovered. Struggled in school, dropped out, now works to support the family.
- **Father** — Farmer.
- **Mother** — Housewife.
- **Meera (24)** — Childhood friend, now a sharp trader. First adult love interest / ally. Appears at the end of Chapter 1.
- **Ananya (25)** — Small YouTuber. Joins in Chapter 2.

## The System — Dream Merge System
- A blue screen only Aarav can see.
- Quests give coins, skills and power.
- First power: **Eyes of Value** — he can see the hidden value in things: a stock, a person, a monster.
- Second power: **OP Learning** — he masters in hours what takes others years.
- Rule: the system only rewards action. No quest, no power.
